# Limitations

- The required demo uses deterministic replay. It does not measure a live model's task-solving ability.
- The optional GPT-5.6 provider boundary is not exercised in the no-credential validation path.
- The public eval suite is intentionally small and portable. It is not a TerminalBench leaderboard, private benchmark, or claim of unrestricted Aether promotion.
- The demo task is a clean artifact task. Messy files, long-running services, recovery, and dependency installation are represented as future example/eval surfaces rather than hidden claims.
- Evidence timestamps are useful for reconstruction but are not proof of ordering across machines. The verifier's filesystem and subprocess observations are the acceptance evidence.
- The Devpost deadline was July 21, 2026 at 5:00pm PDT; any package work after that deadline is clearly labeled as post-deadline verification or packaging.
